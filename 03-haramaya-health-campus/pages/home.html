Clinical Practice Ground

Hiwot Fana Comprehensive Specialized Hospital

Our premier 800-bed university teaching hospital provides tertiary medical services to over 5 million people across Eastern Ethiopia, offering our medical students unmatched hands-on clinical exposure from year one.

  • 24/7 Emergency & Critical Care Center
  • Modern Surgical Theaters & Intensive Care
  • Specialized Oncology & Maternal Care Units
