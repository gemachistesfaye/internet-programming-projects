Scientific Discovery

Health Sciences Research & Publications

Advancing evidence-based medicine through translational clinical studies, public health epidemiology, and maternal-infant health initiatives.

Key Research Themes

Major Research Centers & Units

Collaborating with national and international health bodies to solve pressing healthcare challenges in the region.

Infectious & Tropical Diseases

Investigating transmission dynamics, diagnostic markers, and treatment outcomes for Tuberculosis, Malaria, HIV/AIDS, and emerging respiratory pathogens.

  • Drug-resistant TB Surveillance
  • Malaria Vector Resistance
  • Molecular Epidemiology Lab

Maternal, Neonatal & Child Health

Conducting community cohort studies to reduce maternal mortality, evaluate neonatal resuscitation protocols, and improve infant nutritional interventions.

  • Harar Health & Demographic Surveillance
  • Kangaroo Mother Care Outcomes
  • Child Nutrition & Stunting Studies

Antimicrobial Resistance & Pharmacology

Monitoring hospital-wide antibiotic utilization, tracking multidrug-resistant bacterial strains, and discovering active phytomedicinal compounds.

  • Hospital Stewardship Programs
  • Ethnobotanical Drug Discovery
  • Pharmacovigilance Network
