Institutional Profile

Pioneering Healthcare Education & Patient Care

Established in 1996, the College of Health and Medical Sciences in Harar has grown into one of Ethiopia's premier medical training centers.

Our Vision & Mission

Dedicated to Medical Rigor and Community Service

Our mission is to improve human health through high-standard medical education, compassionate clinical practice, problem-solving biomedical research, and active community outreach across the Horn of Africa.

  • Excellence: Maintaining international standards in healthcare training.
  • Compassion: Putting patient dignity at the core of all clinical practice.
  • Integrity: Upholding the highest ethical scientific and clinical standards.
