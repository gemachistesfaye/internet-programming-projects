Coursework Overview & Academic Focus

This practicum project portfolio tracks laboratory implementations undertaken as part of the Information Science curriculum. Exercises span from elementary structural markup to modern responsive design systems, API-driven interactions, and component-based styling paradigms.

The curriculum emphasizes accessible digital experiences, robust DOM manipulation, standards-compliant HTML5 semantic architecture, and responsive layouts capable of seamless rendering across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.
